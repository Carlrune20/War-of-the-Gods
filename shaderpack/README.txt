War of the Gods shaders
=======================

An OptiFine 1.7.10 shader pack made for the War of the Gods mod, with a look of its own for each map: Misthalin and
Forinthry, the Wilderness. Pick it in Options > Video Settings > Shaders.

Written from scratch for the mod and licensed like it, under the GNU GPL 3.0:
https://github.com/Carlrune20/War-of-the-Gods
